diffusion model, hardness/submodularity and RIS guarantees, real graph
topologies with controlled behavior parameters, and empirical gains that are
network dependent rather than uniformly significant.

## Recommended strategy

1. Primary conservative target: EDBT 2027 cycle 3. Use the regular research
   track, cut the manuscript to 12 content pages, add the mandatory Artifacts
   section, and foreground model/estimator correctness rather than universal
   empirical dominance.
2. Aggressive immediate target: WSDM 2027. This is the best topical fit, but
   only if a self-contained nine-page version can be produced without hiding
   the proof and experimental limitations. Do not describe Findings acceptance
   as guaranteed or treat it as a substitute for main-track quality.
3. Realistic C-level fallback: monitor PAKDD 2027 and ADMA 2027. Their official
   2027 CFPs were not verified as of this check, so no deadline is recorded.
4. Do not prioritize ICDE or ICDT in the current framing. ICDE needs a real
   data-engineering contribution; ICDT needs substantially deeper foundational
   database theory.
